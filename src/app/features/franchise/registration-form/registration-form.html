Registration fee

Onboard & bid for zones

Complete the franchise application and pay ₹5,000 registration. ₹4,000 is refundable toward your first winning zone bid; ₹1,000 covers processing.

Example: Zone base ₹2,50,000, current bid ₹2,55,000, minimum next bid ₹2,60,000 (₹5,000 increments). Your ₹5,000 registration adjusts what you pay at settlement; the leaderboard still shows the full bid amount. If you do not win, refundable rules apply as per agreement.

If you bid again on another zone after a refund scenario, you typically pay the full bid amount— adjustment applies on the first winning bid as per policy.
