Franchise amount logic

How franchise amount is determined

Franchise amount is not a separate extra charge. It is the sum of all zones you win in bidding (single zone or multiple zones).

  • check_circle If you win one zone, franchise amount equals that won zone bid amount.
  • check_circle If you win multiple zones, franchise amount equals the total of all won zone bid amounts.

How you earn — seven services

Earnings come from completed appointments across AIM services. Revenue is shared as a percentage between company, you (franchiser), and the service provider (doctor, nurse, hospital, ambulance, etc.). Illustrative split below (5% / 10% / 85%)—actual percentages may vary by service type and agreement.
